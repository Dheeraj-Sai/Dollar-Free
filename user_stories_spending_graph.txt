Feature: View a Spending Graph

User story
As a user, I want to see my spending by category in a terminal graph so that
I can quickly compare where my money went on a given day.

Acceptance criteria
1. Choosing option 5 asks for a date in mm/dd/yyyy format.
2. The graph draws one bar per category, labeled along the bottom.
3. Categories with nothing spent on that date are left off the graph.
4. The y-axis goes up to 200 by default.
5. If any category's total for that date is over 200, the y-axis switches to
   go up to 1000 instead.
6. A single category's bar is capped at 1000 even if the real total is
   higher.
7. A clear message is shown when nothing was spent on the chosen date.
8. A blank or badly formatted date shows a useful error message without
   crashing.
